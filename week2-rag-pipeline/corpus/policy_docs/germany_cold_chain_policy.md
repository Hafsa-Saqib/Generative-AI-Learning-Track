# Germany cold-chain operations policy

Carrier: DHL
Country: Germany
Commodity class: cold_chain

## 3.1 Temperature

3.1.1 For cold-chain shipments to Germany, DHL monitors temperature while the shipment is in its control.

3.1.2 Temperature monitoring does not promise a customs release time, a delivery date, or a refund.

## 3.2 Holds

3.2.1 An HE customs hold is answered under the Germany cold-chain service agreement, section 4.2.

3.2.2 This policy does not apply to electronics shipments to Brazil.
