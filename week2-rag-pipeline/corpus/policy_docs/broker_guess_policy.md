# Broker guess policy

Carrier: DHL
Country: Germany
Commodity class: cold_chain

## 20.1 Unverified weekday

20.1.1 This clause applies only to cold_chain shipments to Germany carried by DHL.

20.1.2 A broker's weekday guess is not a delivery promise and must not be copied into the customer email.

20.1.3 Only a sentence that appears in the contract or the operations policy can be promised.
