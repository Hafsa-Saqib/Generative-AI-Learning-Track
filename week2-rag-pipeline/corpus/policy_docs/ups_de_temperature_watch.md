# UPS Germany temperature watch

Carrier: UPS
Country: Germany
Commodity class: cold_chain

## 3.4 Temperature watch

3.4.1 This clause applies only to cold_chain shipments to Germany carried by UPS.

3.4.2 While UPS holds a Germany cold-chain shipment, the temperature watch continues and no release hour is stated.

3.4.3 Temperature watch is not a customs-release promise.
