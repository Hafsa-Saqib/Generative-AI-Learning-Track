# Weekend customs-clock policy

Carrier: DHL
Country: Germany
Commodity class: general

## 19.1 Weekend desks

19.1.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to Germany carried by DHL.

19.1.2 Weekend customs desks do not start the 48-hour clock.

19.1.3 A Saturday arrival is not treated as hour one of a customs-hold timer.
