# Lithium battery handling policy

Carrier: DHL
Country: France
Commodity class: electronics

## 18.1 Air departure

18.1.1 This clause applies only to electronics shipments to France carried by DHL.

18.1.2 Lithium batteries are not promised an air departure while the dangerous-goods check is open.

18.1.3 The policy does not give a flight number or a delivery date.
