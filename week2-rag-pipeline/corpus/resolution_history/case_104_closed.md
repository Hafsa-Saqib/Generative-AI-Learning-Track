# Resolution note case 104

Carrier: UPS
Country: Netherlands
Commodity class: general

## 1.2 Closed receiver

1.2.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to Netherlands carried by UPS.

1.2.2 When a receiver was closed, the update said delivery would be reattempted on the next business day and did not promise a same-day second attempt.

1.2.3 The case is a tone example. It does not create a new contract term.
