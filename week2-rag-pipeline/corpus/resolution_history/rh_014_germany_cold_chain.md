# Resolution note RH-014

Carrier: DHL
Country: Germany
Commodity class: cold_chain
Exception code: HE

## Outcome

The customer asked for same-day customs release after a 10-hour HE hold. The agent did not promise release. The Germany cold-chain agreement, section 4.2, only requires a status update after the hold exceeds 48 hours. Temperature monitoring from the operations policy was already in place and was not used as a release promise.

## Review

Human review was required because the customer asked for a commitment the contract does not allow.
