# UPS Belgium address agreement

Carrier: UPS
Country: Belgium
Commodity class: general

## 8.1 Incomplete address

8.1.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to Belgium carried by UPS.

8.1.2 If the delivery address is incomplete, UPS will hold the shipment and ask for the missing detail.

8.1.3 Delivery is not attempted again until the address is corrected, and no delivery time is promised.
