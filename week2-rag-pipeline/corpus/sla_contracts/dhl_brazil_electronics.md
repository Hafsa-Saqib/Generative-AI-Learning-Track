# DHL Brazil electronics service agreement

Carrier: DHL
Country: Brazil
Commodity class: electronics

## 5.1 Scope

5.1.1 This agreement applies to electronics shipments to Brazil.

5.1.2 The Germany cold-chain agreement does not apply to these shipments.

## 5.2 Customs hold

5.2.1 An HE customs hold on Brazil electronics has no 48-hour status-update rule from the Germany cold-chain agreement.

5.2.2 This agreement does not promise customs release, a delivery date, or a refund.
