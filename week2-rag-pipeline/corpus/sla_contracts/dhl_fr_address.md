# DHL France address agreement

Carrier: DHL
Country: France
Commodity class: general

## 14.1 Street number

14.1.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to France carried by DHL.

14.1.2 If the street number is missing, DHL will hold the France shipment until the street number is corrected.

14.1.3 No delivery date is promised while the street number is missing.
