# DHL Germany food exam agreement

Carrier: DHL
Country: Germany
Commodity class: food

## 4.4 Customs exam

4.4.1 This clause applies only to food shipments to Germany carried by DHL.

4.4.2 For a chilled food shipment in a customs exam, DHL will send a status update and the latest temperature reading.

4.4.3 The email does not predict when the customs exam will end.
