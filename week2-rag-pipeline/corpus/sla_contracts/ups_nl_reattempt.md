# UPS Netherlands reattempt agreement

Carrier: UPS
Country: Netherlands
Commodity class: general

## 6.1 Closed receiver

6.1.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to Netherlands carried by UPS.

6.1.2 If the receiver is closed, UPS will reattempt delivery on the next business day.

6.1.3 This clause does not promise a same-day second attempt.
