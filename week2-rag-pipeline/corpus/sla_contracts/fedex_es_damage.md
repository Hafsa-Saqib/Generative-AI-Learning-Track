# FedEx Spain inspection agreement

Carrier: FedEx
Country: Spain
Commodity class: general

## 16.1 Damaged carton

16.1.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to Spain carried by FedEx.

16.1.2 If a Spain carton arrives crushed, FedEx will start an inspection within 3 business days.

16.1.3 A refund is not promised before the inspection is finished.
