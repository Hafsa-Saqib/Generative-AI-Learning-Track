# UPS France weather boundary

Carrier: UPS
Country: France
Commodity class: general

## 2.4 Weather boundary

2.4.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to France carried by UPS.

2.4.2 UPS does not borrow the FedEx weather-delay refund rule for a France lane.

2.4.3 No refund is offered from a clause that names a different carrier.
