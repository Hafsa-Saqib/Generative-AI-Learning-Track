# FedEx Germany pickup agreement

Carrier: FedEx
Country: Germany
Commodity class: general

## 3.1 Missed pickup

3.1.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to Germany carried by FedEx.

3.1.2 If a pickup is missed because the shipper dock was closed, FedEx will schedule the next pickup on the following business day.

3.1.3 The email must not blame the customer for the closed dock.
