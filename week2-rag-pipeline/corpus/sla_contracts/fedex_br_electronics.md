# FedEx Brazil electronics agreement

Carrier: FedEx
Country: Brazil
Commodity class: electronics

## 12.1 Scope

12.1.1 This clause applies only to electronics shipments to Brazil carried by FedEx.

12.1.2 An electronics shipment to Brazil does not inherit a Germany cold-chain status update.

12.1.3 This agreement does not promise customs release or a refund.
