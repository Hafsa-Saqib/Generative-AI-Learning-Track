# DHL Germany cold-chain service agreement

Carrier: DHL
Country: Germany
Commodity class: cold_chain

## 4.2 Customs-hold response

4.2.1 This clause applies only to cold-chain shipments to Germany.

4.2.2 If an HE customs hold exceeds 48 hours after the carrier notifies the customer, DHL will send a status update and the next action within 48 hours of that notification.

4.2.3 This clause does not promise customs release, a delivery date, or a refund.

## 4.3 What this agreement does not cover

4.3.1 This agreement does not apply to electronics shipments to Brazil.

4.3.2 This agreement does not promise a same-day customs release.
