# DHL Germany damage agreement

Carrier: DHL
Country: Germany
Commodity class: cold_chain

## 7.1 Refused carton

7.1.1 This clause applies only to cold_chain shipments to Germany carried by DHL.

7.1.2 If a cold-chain carton is refused because it is leaking, DHL will open a damage review within 2 business days.

7.1.3 The first customer message must not promise a replacement or a refund before that review is finished.
