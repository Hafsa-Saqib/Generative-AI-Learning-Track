# UPS Netherlands pharma agreement

Carrier: UPS
Country: Netherlands
Commodity class: pharma

## 9.1 Health certificate

9.1.1 This clause applies only to pharma shipments to Netherlands carried by UPS.

9.1.2 If customs requests a health certificate and the HE hold has exceeded 48 hours, UPS will notify the shipper within 1 business day and name the missing document.

9.1.3 This clause does not promise customs clearance.
