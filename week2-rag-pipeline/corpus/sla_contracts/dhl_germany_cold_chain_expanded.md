# DHL Germany cold-chain warehouse appendix

Carrier: DHL
Country: Germany
Commodity class: cold_chain

This appendix describes warehouse handling. It does not change section 4.2 of the service agreement.

## 40.1 Probe Calibration

40.1.1 The warehouse log for probe calibration is completed once per shift for Germany cold-chain freight.

40.1.2 A missing probe calibration entry is corrected on the same shift sheet and is not a customer delivery promise.

40.1.3 The probe calibration entry does not change the customs-hold response in the service agreement.
