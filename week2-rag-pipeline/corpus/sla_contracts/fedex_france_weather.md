# FedEx France weather-delay agreement

Carrier: FedEx
Country: France
Commodity class: general

## 2.1 Weather delay

2.1.1 This clause applies only to general shipments to France carried by FedEx.

2.1.2 If a departure is cancelled because of weather, FedEx will move the shipment on the next available departure and tell the customer the revised departure.

2.1.3 A weather delay does not create a refund, and this clause does not cover a mechanical breakdown.
