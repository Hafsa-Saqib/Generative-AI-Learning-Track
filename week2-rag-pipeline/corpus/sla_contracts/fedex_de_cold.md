# FedEx Germany cold-chain boundary

Carrier: FedEx
Country: Germany
Commodity class: cold_chain

## 4.8 Status-update boundary

4.8.1 This clause applies only to cold_chain shipments to Germany carried by FedEx.

4.8.2 FedEx does not borrow the DHL 48-hour status-update rule for Germany cold-chain freight.

4.8.3 No status-update promise is copied from the DHL section 4.2 clause.
