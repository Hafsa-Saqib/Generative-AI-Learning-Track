# UPS Netherlands seal agreement

Carrier: UPS
Country: Netherlands
Commodity class: pharma

## 9.4 Broken seal

9.4.1 This clause applies only to pharma shipments to Netherlands carried by UPS.

9.4.2 If the seal is broken, the shipment must not be redelivered until quality review is complete.

9.4.3 The customer message may say redelivery is paused. It may not give a release date.
